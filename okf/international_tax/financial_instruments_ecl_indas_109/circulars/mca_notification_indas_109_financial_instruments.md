# MCA Notification: Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 — Ind AS 109 Financial Instruments

## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Background
Ind AS 109 establishes principles for recognizing, measuring, and reporting financial assets and financial liabilities. It fundamentally replaces the incurred loss impairment model of predecessor Ind AS 39 with a forward-looking Expected Credit Loss (ECL) model, harmonized with International Financial Reporting Standard 9 (IFRS 9).

## 2. Mandatory 3-Stage Impairment Model
1. **Stage 1 (Performing)**:
   - Financial instruments with no significant increase in credit risk (SICR) since initial recognition.
   - Loss allowance measured at an amount equal to **12-month expected credit losses**.
   - Interest revenue calculated on gross carrying amount.

2. **Stage 2 (Under-Performing)**:
   - Instruments that have experienced SICR since initial recognition but are not credit-impaired (typically past due > 30 days).
   - Loss allowance measured at an amount equal to **lifetime expected credit losses**.
   - Interest revenue calculated on gross carrying amount.

3. **Stage 3 (Credit-Impaired / Non-Performing)**:
   - Financial assets that have objective evidence of impairment (typically past due > 90 days or default events).
   - Loss allowance measured at **lifetime expected credit losses**.
   - Interest revenue calculated on net carrying amount (gross minus loss allowance).

## 3. Simplified Approach for Trade Receivables
For trade receivables, contract assets, and lease receivables without a significant financing component:
- The entity is permitted/mandated to measure loss allowance at an amount equal to lifetime ECL at all times.
- A **Provision Matrix** based on historical credit loss experience across defined aging buckets (e.g. Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, >90 days overdue) is utilized, adjusted for forward-looking macroeconomic conditions.

## 4. Indian Direct Tax Treatment (Section 36(1)(vii) vs Ind AS 109)
An ECL impairment provision charged to the Statement of Profit and Loss under Ind AS 109 is **disallowable as a contingent provision** under Indian Income Tax law:
- Under Section 36(1)(vii) read with the Explanation, deduction is allowable ONLY when the debt is actually written off as irrecoverable in the accounts.
- Under Section 40A(9), general provisions for liabilities are disallowed.
- Entities must track a temporary difference and recognize a **Deferred Tax Asset (DTA)** under Ind AS 12 for the disallowed ECL provision until the fiscal year of actual write-off.
