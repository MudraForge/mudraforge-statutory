# MCA Notification: Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 — Ind AS 12 Income Taxes

## 1. Executive Summary & Balance Sheet Approach
Ind AS 12 prescribes the accounting treatment for income taxes. It mandates the **balance sheet liability method**, focusing on temporary differences between the carrying amount of an asset or liability in the balance sheet and its tax base under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## 2. Deferred Tax Assets (DTA) & Statutory Disallowance Bridging
A Deferred Tax Asset is recognized for deductible temporary differences, unabsorbed business losses, and unabsorbed depreciation, subject to the test of probable future taxable profits:
1. **Section 43B(h) (Delayed MSME Payments)**:
   - Amounts disallowed in current fiscal year due to payment beyond 15/45 days are deductible in the subsequent fiscal year when paid.
   - Generates a **Deferred Tax Asset** in the year of disallowance equal to `Disallowed MSME Payable × Enacted Tax Rate`.
2. **Section 36(1)(vii) / Section 40A(9) (Ind AS 109 ECL Loss Allowance)**:
   - ECL credit impairment charged to P&L is disallowed until actual write-off.
   - Generates a **Deferred Tax Asset** equal to `ECL Loss Allowance × Enacted Tax Rate`.
3. **Section 43B General Dues (Bonus, Gratuity, PF, Leave Encashment)**:
   - Unpaid statutory payables create deductible temporary differences and DTA.
4. **Ind AS 116 Lease Liability vs ROU Asset**:
   - Initial and ongoing difference between lease liability and ROU asset carrying amounts yields a net temporary difference.

## 3. Deferred Tax Liabilities (DTL) & Accelerated Tax Depreciation
A Deferred Tax Liability is recognized for all taxable temporary differences:
1. **Section 32 Accelerated Tax Depreciation**:
   - In early years of asset acquisition, tax depreciation under the Section 32 block of assets (WDV method) exceeds book straight-line depreciation under Schedule II.
   - The carrying amount of the asset in books exceeds its tax base, generating a mandatory **Deferred Tax Liability**.

## 4. Effective Tax Rate (ETR) Reconciliation Requirement
Entities must disclose a numerical reconciliation between:
- Accounting profit multiplied by the applicable statutory tax rate (e.g. 25.168% for domestic companies under Section 115BAA); and
- The actual tax expense reported in the Statement of Profit and Loss, explaining tax-exempt income, non-deductible penalties, corporate tax rate differentials, and unrecognized tax losses.
