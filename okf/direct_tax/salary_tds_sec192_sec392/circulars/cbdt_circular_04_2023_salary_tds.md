# Circular No. 04 of 2023: TDS on Salary under Section 192 and Section 115BAC

## Authority & Date
Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Central Board of Direct Taxes, dated 5th April, 2023 (F. No. 370142/06/2023-TPL).

## Summary of Statutory Directives
1. **Default Regime Operation**: Following amendments made by Finance Act, 2023, sub-section (1A) of Section 115BAC of the Income-tax Act, 1961 established the New Tax Regime as the default tax regime for individuals, HUFs, AOPs, and BOIs for Assessment Year 2024-25 onwards.
2. **Employer Duty to Intimate**: Any person responsible for paying salary income under Section 192 shall seek information from each employee regarding their intended tax regime for each financial year.
3. **Presumption of Default**: If an employee fails to intimate their preference, the employer is statutorily mandated to presume that the employee continues under the default New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) and deduct tax accordingly.
4. **Distinction from Final ITR**: Intimation to the employer is for monthly TDS purposes only; the employee retains the right to exercise their final regime choice when filing their annual return under Section 139(1).
