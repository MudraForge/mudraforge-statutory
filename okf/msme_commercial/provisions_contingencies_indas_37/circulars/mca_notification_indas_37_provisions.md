# MCA Notification: Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 — Ind AS 37 Provisions and Contingencies

## 1. Executive Summary & Probability Triangulation Matrix
Ind AS 37 prescribes the accounting criteria for provisions, contingent liabilities, and contingent assets, establishing a clear probability hierarchy:

| Likelihood of Outflow | Probability | Accounting Treatment | Disclosure Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Probable** | $> 50\%$ ("more likely than not") | **Recognize Provision** on Balance Sheet & P&L expense | Full disclosure of movement, nature, and expected timing |
| **Possible** | $5\% - 50\%$ | **Do Not Recognize** on Balance Sheet | **Disclose as Contingent Liability** in Schedule III Notes |
| **Remote** | $< 5\%$ | **Do Not Recognize** | **No Disclosure Required** |

## 2. Statutory Tax Demands (GST, Income Tax, Customs)
Disputed statutory demands must be classified according to legal merits:
1. **Demand Confirmed with High Risk of Adverse Order**:
   - If senior counsel advises that adverse liability is probable ($>50\%$), a provision must be recognized.
2. **Disputed Matter with Strong Legal Grounds under Appeal**:
   - Where the entity has filed an appeal before CIT(Appeals), GST Appellate Authority, or ITAT/CESTAT, and legal counsel assesses that the position is defensible with favorable precedents, the demand is classified as a **Contingent Liability** (disclosed in Notes to Accounts; no balance sheet liability).
3. **Frivolous / Routine SCNs**:
   - Where the risk of an adverse outcome is remote ($<5\%$), no disclosure is required.

## 3. Best Estimate and Discounting
- The amount recognized as a provision must be the best estimate of the expenditure required to settle the present obligation at the end of the reporting period.
- Where the effect of the time value of money is material (e.g. disputes spanning $> 12$ months), the amount of a provision is the **present value** of the expenditures expected to be required to settle the obligation, discounted using a pre-tax rate.
- The unwinding of the discount is recognized as a finance cost in profit or loss.
