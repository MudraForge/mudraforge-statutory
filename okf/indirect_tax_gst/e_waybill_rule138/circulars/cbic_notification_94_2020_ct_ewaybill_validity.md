# Notification No. 94/2020 - Central Tax: CGST (Fourteenth Amendment) Rules, 2020

## Summary of Statutory Amendments
Notification No. 94/2020-Central Tax issued by the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs on 22 December 2020 introduced significant anti-evasion and logistical speed enhancements to the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017 with effect from 1 January 2021.

## Amendment of Rule 138(10) (E-Way Bill Validity Distance)
In the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, in rule 138, in sub-rule (10), in the Table:
1. Against serial number 1, in column (2), for the letters and figures "100 km.", the letters and figures "200 km." shall be substituted;
2. In column (3), for the words "One day in cases other than Over Dimensional Cargo", the words "One day in cases other than Over Dimensional Cargo or multimodal shipment in which at least one leg involves transport by ship" shall be substituted;
3. Against serial number 2, in column (2), for the letters and figures "100 km.", the letters and figures "200 km." shall be substituted.

### Statutory Impact on Supply Chain and Logistics
With effect from 1 January 2021, the validity of an E-Way Bill is calculated as:
- **Normal Cargo**: 1 day for every 200 km or part thereof (doubled from previous 100 km threshold).
- **Over Dimensional Cargo (ODC) or multimodal shipment involving ship**: 1 day for every 20 km or part thereof.
- **Expiry Timing**: The day ends at 12:00 midnight of the day immediately following the date of generation.
- **Extension Facility**: Under the second proviso to Rule 138(10), the transporter may extend the validity period after updating details in Part B within 8 hours prior to or after expiry.
