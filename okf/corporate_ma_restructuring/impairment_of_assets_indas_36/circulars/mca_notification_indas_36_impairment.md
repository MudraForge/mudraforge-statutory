# Ministry of Corporate Affairs — Notification G.S.R. 111(E)

## Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015: Ind AS 36

**Authority**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs, New Delhi  
**Effective Date**: 1st April, 2015  
**Applicability**: Mandatory for all companies governed by Ind AS under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013  

### Statutory Objective
The Central Government notified the **Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 36: Impairment of Assets**, converging with **IAS 36** and aligned with **US GAAP ASC Topics 360 and 350**. Ind AS 36 establishes procedures that an entity applies to ensure that its assets are carried at no more than their recoverable amount.

### Primary Recognition & Measurement Principles

1. **Identifying an Asset That May Be Impaired (Paragraphs 7–17)**
   - External sources of information: significant decline in asset market value, adverse technological/market/economic/legal changes, market interest rate increases reducing discount rates, carrying amount of company net assets exceeding market capitalization.
   - Internal sources of information: obsolescence or physical damage, discontinued operations or restructuring, economic performance worse than expected.
   - Mandatory annual testing regardless of indicators for goodwill and intangible assets with indefinite useful lives.

2. **Measuring Recoverable Amount (Paragraphs 18–57)**
   - Recoverable amount is the **higher of** an asset's or CGU's **fair value less costs of disposal** and its **value in use**.
   - If either exceeds carrying amount, the asset is not impaired and calculation of the other amount is unnecessary.
   - Value in Use: Reflects estimated future cash flows, expectations about possible variations, time value of money (pre-tax discount rate), and price for bearing uncertainty.

3. **Recognizing and Allocating an Impairment Loss (Paragraphs 58–108)**
   - If recoverable amount < carrying amount, reduce carrying amount to recoverable amount and recognize an **impairment loss immediately in profit or loss**.
   - For a Cash-Generating Unit (CGU), the impairment loss shall be allocated:
     1. First, to reduce the carrying amount of any **goodwill** allocated to the CGU.
     2. Second, to the **other assets of the unit pro-rata** on the basis of the carrying amount of each asset.
   - Constraint: In allocating an impairment loss, the carrying amount of an asset shall not be reduced below the highest of its fair value less costs of disposal (if measurable), its value in use (if determinable), and zero.

4. **Reversing an Impairment Loss (Paragraphs 109–125)**
   - An impairment loss recognized for **goodwill shall never be reversed** in a subsequent period.
   - For other assets, an impairment loss recognized in prior periods is reversed only if there has been a change in estimates used to determine the recoverable amount. The increased carrying amount cannot exceed the carrying amount that would have been determined (net of amortization/depreciation) had no impairment loss been recognized.
